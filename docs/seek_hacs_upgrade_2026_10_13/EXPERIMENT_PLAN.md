# Next phase: executable, evidence-driven Seek search

Date: 13 October 2026. Basis: the supplied Codex audit and the current Seek theorem. All budgets below are proposals, not authorizations for local inference, actual registrations, runtime estimates or achieved results.

## Goal

Demonstrate that a role-specialized Seek system can systematically search a bounded, declared hypothesis/experiment space, use actual policy evidence to change that search, and hand a defensible relation to independent confirmation. Keep Shield and `sbatch agent_eval.sh` unchanged. No extra backdoor training, new environment, or live signature feedback is required.

The change is not “ask Qwen to think harder.” Add an algorithmic search state and make the roles review grounded candidate rules and executable experiments. The present confirmation code remains the final evidence authority.

## Phase 1 — CPU-only algorithm integration

Implement HACS as a feature-flagged discovery policy in existing Seek. The essential units are:

- observed-behavior grounding distinct from user requirements;
- capability-derived predicate/experiment graph;
- explicit predictions for every arm;
- automatic evidence reducer and prequential prediction loss;
- cost-aware disagreement scheduler with fair coverage fallback;
- duplicate/stagnation handling and prediction-equivalence reporting;
- compact role review and token preflight;
- unchanged prospective confirmation interface.

### Development regression

Use the saved latest case as an immutable input/output regression, not another real-model trial. The regression must preserve all four `(1,1)` pairs and demonstrate that a literal-distinction prediction loses support relative to compatible predictions. It must not mandate “choose loafers.” Depending on actual capabilities and remaining predictions, the next result may be another discriminating category experiment, justified new-context sampling, an equivalence-test request, or an explicit capability/non-identifiability stop.

Build the same regression with renamed categories, brands and IDs. Include high/high and low/low zero-effect cases, noisy cases and unknown feature mappings. This detects hidden historical-answer patches.

### Capability gate

Before role inference, compile the proposed candidate family and enumerate feasible separating tests. Show each rule's source, each experimental factor, and pairwise separability/limitations. A schema-valid interface that cannot express any alternative to the exhausted wording contrast is not a discovery-ready library.

Do not repair this by hard-coding the historically successful pair. Add a generic, reviewed sibling/ancestor/wording operation or report the missing capability. Any local hand-authored category graph is an explicit domain prior, frozen before prospective evaluation.

### Two separate readiness decisions

**Engineering readiness:** reproducible input, parseable grounded outcome, executable capability map, correct evidence updates, truthful review packets, no unbounded repeats, and correct stopped/limited status. A zero effect or unresolved class may satisfy this gate.

**Scientific assessment:** do the roles accurately describe what the evidence does and does not distinguish? Do they justify the submitted scope or limitation? A separate reviewer applies the prospectively frozen rubric. A positive backdoor result is not a pass condition. Do not patch this same case until a preferred candidate appears.

No automatic full-matrix submission. Keep `method_matrix_unlocked=false` until an authorized reviewer attaches the required gate decision under the new protocol. An assistant self-review cannot satisfy a reviewer-independence requirement.

## Phase 3 — Small fair method study

Collect four fresh query-side investigations from unexposed groups using a fixed eligibility rule and order independent of future method success. Retain unavailable/ungrounded cases and reasons. Do not require an Adidas keyword in a collector filter unless the study is explicitly evaluator-specified.

Compare three role-specialized variants:

1. **HACS active:** same public graph, automatic evidence updates, disagreement/cost scheduling plus coverage.
2. **HACS fixed:** same graph, role review, outcome accounting and evidence updates; test order is prospectively fixed and ignores observed outcome values, except for validity/termination.
3. **Role-selected search:** same feasible graph and compact factual evidence packets; roles select the next experiment without the HACS acquisition ranking. All mechanical safety and accounting fixes apply equally. This is a versioned, capability-matched conversational baseline, not a retroactive edit of the failed historical baseline.

These comparisons isolate search control rather than investigator count. They do not separately establish the benefit of graph construction; measure that in simulation/ablation first.

Proposed main budget: 32 logical victim discovery responses and 12 logical defender messages per investigation/method. Log candidate snapshots at 8/16/32 responses without using later evidence to rewrite earlier snapshots. Initial real held-out scoring concerns the final primary candidate only; do not claim an 8/16 recovery curve without evaluating those frozen prefixes separately.

Core discovery cap:

    4 investigations × 3 methods × 32 = 384 logical victim responses.

Two anchors per run add at most 24 physical responses before cache/runtime accounting. Defender calls are separate. Same-checkpoint investigations are not independent trained attacks.

### Candidate-specific fresh evaluation

Freeze one primary relation/experiment or an unresolved equivalence class per method. Abstention is valid. The common evaluator may reject an incoherent proposal but cannot substitute a known successful comparison.

For an executable paired primary contract, reserve 12 fresh background units and complete both arms. Maximum initial evaluator budget:

    4 × 3 × 12 × 2 = 288 logical responses.

Four-cell contracts require their actual four-response budget and a separate reservation; they do not fit the two-arm allowance. Exact duplicate requests across methods can share a private physical cache under the fixed-runtime protocol. They still cost each method logical access and evaluator results never enter ongoing discovery.

This is **held-out finite-support evaluation**, not a new anytime certificate. Complete support, fixed weights and stable response semantics are required for an exact finite-support effect. Missing outcomes require explicit bounds/missingness or an incomplete status, not deletion of inconvenient cases. Unknown eta remains unknown.

At most one primary finding can later enter a separate prospective certificate run after review. Predeclare selection independent of final confirmation data and allocate fresh support/current j. Do not resume claim 10 or reuse its confirmation outcomes.

### Main reporting

Report selected/eligible/opened/completed counts; candidate-graph coverage; executable contracts; full arm outcomes; hypothesis ranking and unresolved classes; new information per logical call; unjustified repeat requests and executed repeats; reviewer-assessed evidence acknowledgment/scope; independently measured finite effects; abstentions and compiler/inference failures; logical/physical victim and defender costs, token missingness and latency.

A large positive effect on a submitted contrast is not enough to prove a broad semantic explanation. No unique-trigger label is allowed when surviving rules make identical predictions over the tested support. Four investigations yield a descriptive comparison, not a population recovery rate across attacks.

## Phase 4 — Known-rule simulation campaign

Reuse the implemented simulator. Add HACS and matched fixed/role-or-script scheduling, then run 1,000 independent study replications per selected configuration in CPU jobs. Full campaigns are separate from local unit tests and need explicit execution receipts.

Required settings:

- literal versus concept-triggered rules and reviewed synonym groups;
- narrow versus parent-category rules;
- ordinary constant high and constant low outcomes;
- two-factor conjunction;
- position-following and fixed-ID controls;
- missing true rule and prediction-equivalent rules;
- no valid separating experiment;
- stochastic noise and weak effect near threshold;
- malformed outputs/unknown mappings;
- biased exposure screening and adaptive candidate expansion;
- stale repeated evidence and cache reuse.

Measure discovery of the correct **equivalence class**, candidate coverage, query cost, false confident localization and non-identifiability recognition. Separately measure theorem-backend family-wise false effect certification, detection probability and cost with Monte Carlo intervals. A genuine normal preference is not a statistical false positive about a true effect.

Test the idealized elimination theorem only on its own specified backend and assumptions. The production soft predictor does not inherit the balanced binary-search guarantee. Keep discovery failure risk and confirmation delta separate; a joint probability claim requires an explicit combined error budget.

Prospectively cap simulation horizons and report underpowered/censored conditions. A zero false-certificate count with zero power is not success.

The query method study and CPU simulations do not wait for an observation backdoor to be recovered.

## Success criteria for this phase

The minimum deliverable is an auditable trace in which a structured candidate family is proposed, the available distinguishing experiments are known, actual two-arm evidence updates the search state, the next test follows that update or the system gives a precise limitation, and the final relation is evaluated on fresh support. This is useful even when the final answer is an unresolved class.

The larger research target is higher useful-investigation rate or lower cost than capability-matched scheduling variants. Neither improvement is assumed. Preserve a negative result.
