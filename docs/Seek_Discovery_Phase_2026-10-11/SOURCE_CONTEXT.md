## New proposals, not reported facts

The four-investigation method pilot, 16-response discovery cap, 12-pair finite evaluator, 120-response observation diagnostic plus anchors, optional crossed query table and simulation campaign design are recommendations for the next phase. They are not recovered experimental parameters or known future results.

A useful completed phase may contain null or position-dominated findings. No experiment is authorized to chase an expected sign, manufacture semantic certainty, force an investigation output, or silently change the frozen test to obtain a certificate.
