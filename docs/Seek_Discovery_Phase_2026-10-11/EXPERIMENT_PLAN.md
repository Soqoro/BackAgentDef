## 4. P1 — incident-led query Seek: the primary research deliverable

### 4.1 Prepare a prospective incident bank

Start with **four query-side investigations** from distinct eligible source groups where available. Use genuine Shield rejections when available; otherwise retain `raw_audit` or `constructed_incident` provenance. Do not relabel a raw proposal as an executed attack or a Shield failure.

A public incident contains the user request/contract, exact consumed context, raw proposal, action mapping and actual rejection reason. It does not contain claim 10 results, attack-construction labels, private checkpoint descriptions, prewritten sneaker/loafer hypotheses or expected effects. Checkpoint aliases exposed to roles should be opaque. The target brand may legitimately be inferred from the observed proposal, not injected from evaluator metadata.

Select incidents using an explicit, outcome-independent ordering among eligible records. An incident-driven study is conditional on its incident-selection rule; do not claim performance over all tasks or all backdoors. Prefer genuinely unseen groups. Legacy pilot incidents may be used for debugging with a development label, not silently recycled as unseen method evaluation.

Add ordinary-error or no-cue controls when authentic eligible examples are available. Missing real negative controls are reported, not synthesized and labeled real. Simulations supply fully known negative cases separately.

### 4.2 Require actual agent ownership

Use the existing Goal, State and Action roles. The initial hypothesis, semantic scope, outcome and revisions must be attributable to their authors. A deterministic compiler can instantiate a declared operator; it cannot replace a lexical hypothesis with sneakers-versus-watches/loafers while retaining an autonomous-discovery label.

An unsupported agent request produces a structured error identifying the missing operator or ambiguity. The agents may explicitly revise it. No hidden successful fallback is allowed. Keep the full rejected and revised proposals.

The common operator library can support wording substitutions, explicit category comparisons, observation ordering, IDs, label assignments and null comparisons. It must describe capabilities without naming the correct explanation for an incident or sorting operators by historical success. This evaluates **library-constrained incident-led investigation**, not unrestricted causal discovery.

### 4.3 Compare three protocols, not investigator counts

- **Adaptive Seek:** the three roles select the next approved experiment from accumulated evidence.
- **Fixed schedule:** the same roles interpret a prospectively fixed schedule over the same admissible library; no outcome-driven reordering. The schedule is generated from public incident structure and a frozen seed, not chosen deliberately to avoid useful probes.
- **Discussion only:** the same public incident and library; no diagnostic victim calls. It returns a candidate contract or abstention, not a self-validated signature.

Use **16 logical victim-response calls per investigation** for each probe-using method initially. A two-arm probe costs two calls; a four-cell comparison costs four. Precomputed cache hits still consume the logical information budget. Cap defender logical calls at 24 per investigation, with at most two attempts per call; invalid attempts consume budget and remain in accounting.

Four investigations × two probe-using methods × 16 responses = **128 logical discovery responses** maximum. Up to 12 method runs × 24 defender logical calls = **288 defender logical calls**; do not confuse a cap with actual cost. Stop naturally when a method freezes its candidate or exhausts resources.

As a first engineering checkpoint, complete one real adaptive investigation before launching the full method matrix. This prefix belongs to the same predeclared run only when its configuration and stopping rules were frozen; any prompt repair creates a new development version, not retroactive confirmation.

### 4.4 Independent common evaluation

Each method submits **one primary frozen contract** at the stated budget, or abstains. The evaluator validates that submitted contract as written. Do not convert all outputs to the known sneaker/loafer comparison.

Use **12 new paired backgrounds per executable primary contract** for a first held-out finite-support evaluation. Freeze the generator, full support, weights, scorer and acceptance convention before victim outcomes. Pair the same background draws across methods only when their rendered semantics are compatible; otherwise retain common sampling rules and report the supports separately.

With four investigations and three methods, the ceiling is **4 × 3 × 12 × 2 = 288 logical evaluator responses** for paired contracts on the investigated checkpoint. A larger-arity contract requires an explicit equalized cost plan; do not charge it as a pair. Identical exact prompts can share a private physical cache but each method pays its logical access cost. Do not expose evaluator results to any unfinished method. Zero claims are evaluated for an abstaining method; denominator remains four selected investigations.

This small complete support is a **held-out finite benchmark**, not a new anytime certificate and not a proof of the intended semantic concept. Return its implemented effect, complete-support coverage, strict usability/missingness results and interpretation status. Unknown eta remains unknown. A submitted overbroad concept may receive `partial_scope_supported` even when its exact renderer has a large effect.

Later statistical confirmation is optional and separately registered. Prefer at most one clearly specified agent-origin claim for that extension, with a power check at the actual current j. Do not run long confirmation separately for every method merely to rank their exploratory candidates.

### 4.5 Results and study limits

Report per investigation and protocol: executable primary contract, agent-origin versus controller-assisted/evaluator origin, experiment validity, held-out implemented effect, scope overclaim, abstention/error, logical/physical victim and defender costs, and revisions grounded in actual probe outcomes.

Use counts such as 3/4, not a population recovery percentage over independently trained attacks. Four investigations on one checkpoint remain a small within-checkpoint study. Neither a strong query relation nor the mere existence of a discussion trace establishes adaptive superiority. Equal results across methods are legitimate; the multi-agent architectural framing does not require superiority to be invented.

## 5. P2 — observation diagnostics: small and separate

### 5.1 First compare the exact old and new consumed inputs (CPU)

Reconstruct prompt/rendering differences between the older label experiment (claims 2/6) and claim 7. Use original frozen sources and exact inputs when available; current approximations are new reconstructions and must be labeled as such.

Inspect system/user/assistant boundaries, preceding thought/action history, page heading, candidate lines, explicit action examples, truncation, displayed order, legal-action order, slot-ID order, label placement and the comparison label. Check whether any parser/sort step turns all apparent choices into the first candidate. Cross-check original raw actions and the separate display-position mapping. A score correction does not authorize altering old generated actions.

The older one-slot label manipulation and the newer two-slot brand swap test different interventions; do not attribute their difference to layout alone without a controlled bridge. Keep broad brand response, category-conditioned response, positional choice and template sensitivity as competing explanations.

### 5.2 Bounded bridge pilot

Propose one reviewed pilot with **four backgrounds × two renderer families × two display orders × two brand assignments × three checkpoints = 96 core responses**.

The renderer families should be obtained from the existing code/snapshots (for example an earlier label-style representation and the newer factorial representation). Render the same reviewed content through both where equivalence is attainable. When content, history or affordances cannot be made comparable, label this as a template bundle contrast, not an isolated layout effect. A missing historical renderer is a prerequisite for that cell, not permission to invent it.

Within each pair, both candidate choices remain available. Swap target and comparison brands between persistent candidate slots; non-brand facts and stable underlying identities remain unchanged. Counterbalance display order independently. Use both slot choice and displayed-position outcomes, with brand-following and fixed-ID summaries. Source-recorded lexical values are used only in the evaluator-specified diagnostic branch, not leaked into the method-study prompts.

Do not automatically add another sneaker-by-brand factorial: claim 7 already tested one such design. First learn whether changing renderer/opportunity/context explains its positional result. Category-conditioned follow-up is justified only by a new registered question, not by assuming that sneaker presence must be the sole trigger.

### 5.3 A small selection-competence control

Add **four coherent neutral-brand backgrounds × two display orders × three checkpoints = 24 core responses**. Give an explicit objective with an unambiguous valid choice, such as the cheaper of two otherwise suitable items. The cheaper item must appear first and second equally. Product details and task instructions are hypothetical and labeled constructed.

This asks whether the policies use visible task information under the current interface. It is not a clean-model proof and not an observation-trigger-removal arm. The control must not instruct the victim to avoid position bias or reveal the desired slot; it simply states an ordinary task preference.

Proposed total: **120 core responses**, plus **two preselected replay anchors per checkpoint = 6**, for **126 physical responses** absent cache reuse/failures. Keep identical templates/decoding per frozen cell and do not keep revising prompts until a target-brand effect appears.

### 5.4 Predeclared interpretation and stop rules

- Parsing, mapping, truncation or source-binding failure: freeze the failed version, patch the defect, and create a new pilot. No old-result relabeling.
- Reference and victim both use the intended attribute in neutral controls but show zero brand effect: report a genuine null/descriptive finding for that support. It is not grounds to alter the renderer until a positive effect emerges.
- All models remain position-dominated, including neutral controls: report that this diagnostic format does not demonstrate attribute-sensitive choice; do not launch a large interaction confirmation.
- Brand effect varies with renderer: report renderer-conditioned sensitivity and evaluate any selected claim on untouched support. Do not pool the two formats into a universal claim.
- A useful observation relationship is absent: leave observation diagnosis unresolved and continue the query-method and simulation work.

Allow at most one technically justified pilot repair before a written review gate. Structural correctness gates do not depend on attack-effect sign. Any performance threshold used to select a later renderer must be frozen in advance and based on neutral task competence, with all rejected designs and costs reported.

The motivating attack paper illustrates Adidas appearing in observations; the owner describes the implemented checkpoint as sneaker-associated. The new study must not silently treat either description as proven learned ground truth. Keep the precise operator and measured outcome in every claim.

## 8. Inference, evidence isolation and resource controls

### Preserve existing statistical semantics

The installed anytime radius for a score in [-1,1] is

`r(j,n) = sqrt((2/n) * log(2*j*(j+1)*n*(n+1)/delta))`.

For a linear contrast of [0,1] cell outcomes, compute its range from coefficients. Radius scale is half the range width: ordinary pair 1; four-cell interaction 2; difference of interactions 4. Keep strict lower-bound > threshold. At unknown eta, only an implemented-effect interpretation is available; no semantic certificate.

Keep diagnostics, exploratory probes, finite-support evaluations, and confirmation streams separate. A 12-background pilot must not acquire a statistical badge from the theorem. A census must cover its complete declared support with stable deterministic responses and all scorable cells before returning a complete finite-support effect. Unknown semantic meaning is separate from stochastic uncertainty.

Do not impute malformed or missing actions as zero. Record coverage and failure type; retain the frozen missingness rule. Missing cells block a complete census, while any conservative partial bound must be explicitly declared. Do not discard only unfavorable or invalid pairs and call the remaining data a random sample.

### Keep computation efficient and accountable

Victim tasks use one model resident per worker. Qwen discussion uses the already staged larger-memory environment; victim queries can be serviced via resumable jobs or a private oracle cache. One-GPU sequential execution remains supported; do not require simultaneous residency of all models.

Cache keys must bind checkpoint, tokenizer, consumed input tokens, generation/runtime configuration and relevant protocol version. Return raw responses only for requested discovery inputs. A cache hit consumes logical budget but not another physical generation. In stochastic protocols, reusing one sampled response is not a fresh independent draw.

Matched seeds across deterministic runs do not create independent datasets. Resume cursors, failed attempts, anchors and method costs remain explicit. Agent-proposed hypothesis text and errors must not be truncated out of audit logs.
